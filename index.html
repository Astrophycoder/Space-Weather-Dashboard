SPACE WEATHER
IS A COUPLED SYSTEM. Solar output drives a stream of plasma and magnetic field through interplanetary space. When that flow couples with Earth's magnetosphere, energy is transferred into the near-Earth environment.

Watch the chain: solar activity → solar wind → magnetic field → geomagnetic response.
WHEN IT
REACHES
EARTH
01 / SPACECRAFT

Energetic particles and atmospheric expansion can affect spacecraft electronics, radiation exposure and orbital drag.

02 / NAVIGATION

Ionospheric disturbances can introduce errors into GNSS positioning and timing.

03 / RADIO

Solar X-rays and ionospheric disturbances can disrupt high-frequency radio propagation.
